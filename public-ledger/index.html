Pruning context for preference answers

Permalink #

On 30 matched preference questions, we compared the full retrieved top ten with a context containing only the retrieved passages labeled as support. A blinded rubric then graded the same 60 saved answers.

Pruning won two, lost two, and tied 23 of the 27 pairs with decisive calibration grades. We parked this variant and kept retrieval unchanged.
